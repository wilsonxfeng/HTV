# ReMotion garden artwork

## Included original artwork

Created for this project with OpenAI's built-in image generation tool on October 17, 2026. These are generated outputs, not downloaded third-party art or assets claimed to be CC0. Use is governed by the applicable OpenAI service terms. No third-party artist attribution is attached. Copyright protection for generated output may vary; no exclusivity is claimed.

Local files in `public/assets/garden/`:
- `background.webp`: original cream, butter-yellow and sage gouache garden clearing.
- `stage-0.webp` through `stage-4.webp`: seed/soil, sprout, leafy plant, bud and daisy bloom. Cropped and optimized from a generated six-cell transparent asset sheet.
- `watering-can.webp`: sage watering can from the same sheet.
- `flower-1.webp`, `flower-2.webp`: original pink tulip and yellow sunflower variants.
- `soil-and-seed.webp`: close crop of the seed and soil patch.
- `paper-texture.webp`: cream paper crop from the generated background.

Art direction: warm gouache, natural brush texture, cream paper, adult botanical journal. The supplied photographs and paintings were inspected only as a moodboard; no pixels from those works are included.

Generation prompts: original garden clearing with open foreground; six isolated consistent growth stages and watering can on transparent ground; two matching isolated tulip/sunflower variants. Source images were produced with built-in imagegen, then locally cropped and encoded as WebP. No external asset hotlinks are needed.
